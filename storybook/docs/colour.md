## What the guide says

Every value is defined in OKLCH so lightness steps read evenly. Neutrals are tinted toward the brand hue — never pure grey. Themes are semantic: swap the semantic layer and the primitives stay put.

What a component asks for, not what a colour happens to be. The same role resolves differently in each theme.

Five elevation steps. In dark mode, higher means lighter — never a heavier shadow.

For density, catchments and isoline bands. Seven stops, one hue, rising lightness.

For bearing, aspect and phase. Eight stops, equal lightness, hue carries the encoding, wraps seamlessly from 360° back to 0°.

For hazard, density and continuous surfaces. Six stops, non-monotonic lightness — hot, not ordered.
